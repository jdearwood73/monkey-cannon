Prototype rule guesses
  • Everyone starts with the starting money you pick and one Roll Again card.
  • At the Banana Farm you roll the Banana Die, buy, then roll and move on the same turn.
  • Moving backward stops at the Banana Farm (you can't pass it).
  • Monkey Business cards are free: draw one on a Monkey Business space. Keep cards go in your hand (up to 3) and are played on your own turn, one per turn; the rest happen right away. A Banana Shield blocks the next card aimed at you.
  • The Banana Farm and the Surplus Market are hard stops — everyone stops there. Land on either with your exact roll and you get a free banana.
  • CDs are only bought or cashed when you land on a Bank or arrive at the Banana Farm, and always cash at full value. A CD must be held at least one turn before it can be cashed.
  • Loans: borrow or repay any time (one loan at a time); you can't buy letter cards while you owe.
  • "Sell for Loss" is all or nothing. You can't buy a letter you already have.
  • "Nearest Bank" means the next Bank ahead. Ad cards aren't in yet (no Ad space on the board).
